# Advisor Profile 100% Completion Guide

Ye guide simple language me batati hai ki advisor apni public profile ko `100% complete` kaise kare.

## Completion ka rule kaise kaam karta hai

System total `9 points` check karta hai.

Har point complete hone par score badhta hai.

`100% completion` ke liye sabhi 9 points complete hone chahiye.

## 9 cheezein jo complete honi chahiye

### 1. Profile photo upload ho
- Advisor ke account me proper profile photo honi chahiye.
- Blank ya missing photo par ye point nahi milega.

### 2. Short bio filled ho
- `Short Bio` section me meaningful intro likha hona chahiye.
- Sirf empty chhodne par point nahi milega.

### 3. Total real estate experience filled ho
- `Total Real Estate Experience (Years)` me value honi chahiye.
- Ye market experience hai, company tenure nahi.

### 4. Specialization tags selected hon
- Kam se kam ek `Specialization Tag` select hona chahiye.
- Example: `Luxury Specialist`, `Plot Specialist`, `Investment Advisor`
- Important: ye tags final form me company/CRM aapki specialization aur performance ke hisab se degi.
- Matlab aap jaise-jaise performance doge, waise-waise better tags mil sakte hain.
- Advisor ko sirf relevant profile details fill karni hain; final trusted tag company decide karegi.

### 5. Service areas filled hon
- `Service Areas` me kam se kam ek area hona chahiye.
- Example: `Gomti Nagar, Faizabad Road`

### 6. Why Choose Me filled ho
- `Why Choose Me` section blank nahi hona chahiye.
- Ye customer ko advisor ki value samjhata hai.

### 7. Stats me kuch real data ho
- Inme se koi ek ya zyada field `0` se bada hona chahiye:
- `Successful Closures`
- `Site Visits Handled`
- `Sq.Ft Sold`
- `Happy Families Served`

### 8. Gallery me kam se kam 1 image ho
- Advisor ko gallery me kam se kam ek image upload karni hogi.
- Category kuch bhi ho sakti hai:
- `Achievement`
- `Certificate`
- `With Customer`
- `Booking Moment`
- `Site Visit`
- `Meeting`

### 9. Kam se kam 1 approved review ho
- Public profile completion ke liye kam se kam ek `CRM-approved review` hona chahiye.
- Sirf pending review count nahi hota.

## Important notes

### Company tenure alag hai
- `With company` ya `Company tenure` system khud calculate karta hai.
- Ye advisor fill nahi karta.
- Ye completion score ka part nahi hai.

### Specialization tags company decide karegi
- Aapko tags aapki specialization, role understanding, aur actual performance ke hisab se diye jayenge.
- Example:
- agar aap luxury segment me strong ho to `Luxury Specialist`
- agar aap plots me strong ho to `Plot Specialist`
- agar aap investor deals me better ho to `Investment Advisor`
- Isliye tag sirf form fill karne se nahi, balki performance aur CRM/company review ke baad final hoga.

### Public requested aur approved alag cheezein hain
- `100% complete` hona alag hai
- `Public Requested` alag hai
- `CRM Approval` alag hai

Public page live hone ke liye generally:
- profile kaafi complete ho
- user ne public visibility request ki ho
- CRM approval mila ho

## Fastest way to reach 100%

1. Profile photo upload karo
2. Short bio likho
3. Experience bhar do
4. 2-3 specialization tags select karo
5. 2-3 service areas likho
6. Why Choose Me likho
7. Stats me real numbers bharo
8. Gallery me kam se kam 1 image upload karo
9. Customer se review link share karo aur CRM se review approve karwao

## CRM team ke liye

CRM directly ye help kar sakta hai:
- advisor ke filled fields edit karna
- profile preview dekhna
- profile approve/hide karna
- pending review approve karna
- pending gallery approve karna

Agar advisor ka score `89%` ya `78%` par atka hai, to usually missing item inme se hota hai:
- no profile photo
- no gallery image
- no approved review
- no specialization
- no service area
- no `Why Choose Me`

## Best practice

- Bio real rakho, generic nahi
- Stats fake mat bharo
- Apni profile genuine rakho taaki company aapko sahi specialization tags de sake
- Customer photo upload karte waqt consent lo
- Review link real customer ko bhejo
- CRM approval ke baad hi profile ko live push karo
